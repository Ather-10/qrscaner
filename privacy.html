ScanPro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

ScanPro is a free QR code scanner and generator. Your privacy matters: the app works fully on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. ScanPro has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Camera

The app uses your camera only while the Scan screen is open, to detect QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device and are never saved or uploaded.

Photos

If you choose "Gallery" to scan a QR code from an image, only the image you select is read, on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your scan history, saved QR codes and settings are stored locally on your phone. You can delete items or clear everything inside the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app.

Links and external actions

ScanPro never opens a scanned link automatically. You always see the destination and choose whether to open it. Opening a link, email, phone or map is handled by other apps on your device, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

ScanPro does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
